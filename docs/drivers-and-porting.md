# S5L8947X (`j33iap`) Driver Implementation & Porting Reference

Detailed hardware addresses, driver file paths, kernel patch notes, boot bundle internals, and per-subsystem implementation details moved from the top-level `README.md` and `progress.md`.

## 1. Driver Map & Hardware Details (`S5L8947X` / `j33iap`)

| Block | MMIO / Bus Address | Driver / Source Path | Implementation Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interrupt controller & 24 MHz timer** | `0x3f200000` | `drivers/irqchip/irq-apple-aic1.c` | `aic,1` controller and 24 MHz local timer. Includes `CONFIG_SMP` IPI support and per-CPU register windows for multi-core chips in the `S5L8940X` family. |
| **Watchdog, clocksource & restart** | `0x3f103020` (IRQ 4) | `arch/arm/mach-apple/` | 24 MHz watchdog timer, clocksource, and system restart handler (`apple,s5l8940x-wdt-timer`). |
| **L2 cache (PL310, 512 KB) & ARM NEON** | `0x3e000000` | mainline `cache-l2x0.c`, `arch/arm/mach-apple/s5l8947x.c` | Started from machine init (`PL310_ERRATA_769419` enabled; prefetch bits left off as they measured slower). | **PMGR clock gates & audio NCOs** | `0x3f101008`, `0x3f100110` | `drivers/clk/clk-s5l8940x-pmgr.c` | Linear table of 32-bit peripheral clock gate registers (`0xf` enable, bits `7:4` status); 38 gates claimed across enabled DT nodes on `j33iap`. | **CPU frequency scaling** | `0x3f100000` | `drivers/cpufreq/s5l8947x-cpufreq.c` | `212.5` / `425` / `850 MHz` PMGR performance states. Keeps the peripheral divider constant across states so the 51.3 MHz UART clock does not shift. Registered as a thermal cooling device. |
| **GPIO & Pinctrl (144 pins, 18 ports)** | `0x3fa00000` (IRQ 119) | `drivers/pinctrl/pinctrl-s5l8940x-gpio.c` | Banked pin controller and GPIO IRQ chip (`apple,npins = <144>`). | **I2C 0/1/2** | `0x33200000`..`0x33400000` (IRQ 18..20) | `drivers/i2c/busses/i2c-s5l8940x.c` | Master controller for `i2c0` (PMU at `0x74`), `i2c1` (disabled), and `i2c2` (HDMI DDC EDID at `0x50`). |
| **PMU, regulators & interrupts** | I2C0 `0x74` (GPIO 109 IRQ) | `drivers/mfd/apple-pmu-i2c.c`, `drivers/regulator/apple-pmu-pwrsw.c` | Dialog `D1815` regmap MFD and `regmap-irq` controller: 48 events across 6 read-to-clear registers at `0x01` (masks at `0x0c`). | **Real-time clock (PMU RTC)** | I2C0 `0x74` (PMU event 32) | `drivers/rtc/rtc-apple-pmu.c` | 32-bit seconds counter at `0x4c` plus calendar offset in scratch registers `0x84..0x87` (fraction at `0x95..0x96`). | **Temperature sensors & thermal zone** | I2C0 `0x74` (PMU event 13) | `drivers/hwmon/apple-pmu-hwmon.c` | 12-bit PMU ADC (`0x30..0x32`) reading four 10 kOhm board thermistors (`temp1` SoC, `temp2` Wi-Fi, `temp3` Power supply, `temp4` NAND) via 50 uA source and PMU junction (`temp5`, calibrated from `0x43..0x44`). `soc-thermal` throttles CPU at 70 C and shuts down at 95 C. |

| **USB 2.0 OTG device & HSIC host** | `0x36000000`, `0x36100000` (IRQ 11), `0x36400000` (IRQ 12) | `drivers/phy/phy-apple-s5l-usb.c`, `dwc2` (`0005-*.patch`), `ehci-platform` | OTG port runs `dwc2` in peripheral mode (`g_cdc`: `usb0` + `/dev/ttyGS0`). ## 2. Pre-Kernel Boot Bundle Architecture (`scripts/boot/`)

Device bootloaders in this SoC family enter with caches/MMU in varying states, peripheral clocks gated off, and custom boot arguments rather than a Flattened Device Tree. `scripts/build-bundle.sh` wraps the kernel in a self-relocating pre-kernel loader:

1. **Position-independent entry stub (`scripts/boot/entry-stub.S`)**: Runs from any RAM load address, masks IRQs, flushes/invalidates L1 and PL310 L2 caches, disables the MMU, arms the hardware watchdog (120 s timeout), copies the bundle to `0x80000000`, and jumps there.
2. **Pre-kernel C loader (`scripts/boot/boot-loader.c`)**: Executes in `0x80000000..0x80007fff` (stack down from `0x80007000`, max binary size 24 KB), enables essential PMGR clock gates (`pmgr-init.c`), configures 1080p30 HDMI scanout (`hdmi-boot.h`, 8 MB reserved at `0x9e400000`), renders the polygon boot splash (`gen-splash.py` / `splash.c` from `logo.svg`), and jumps to `zImage` at `0x80008000` with `r2` pointing to the appended `.dtb`.

## 3. Porting Guide for Related `S5L8940X` Devices

1. **Adapt `scripts/boot/`**: Check the physical DRAM base (`0x80000000` on `S5L8947X`) and watchdog address in `entry-stub.S`. Point `simple-framebuffer` at the bootloader's scanout address for early `tty0` output.
2. **Create `.dtsi` / `.dts` under `arch/arm/boot/dts/apple/`**: Use `s5l8947x.dtsi` and `s5l8947x-j33iap.dts` as templates. Start with `aic`, `wdt`, `uart0` (or `usb0`), and `simple-framebuffer`.
3. **Probe hardware from userspace**: Use the built-in static tools (`peek`, `i2cpoke`, `l2bench`) to verify MMIO windows, clock gates, and I2C buses, then enable peripheral DT nodes incrementally.
